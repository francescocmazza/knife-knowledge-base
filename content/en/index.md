# Knife Knowledge Base

An open and practical guide to kitchen knives, created for customer education, commercial training, and clear marketing communication.

The project explains the main ideas behind knife steels, blade construction, geometry, knife types, sharpening, maintenance, and safe use. It aims to make specialist knowledge easier to understand without turning useful simplifications into misleading claims.

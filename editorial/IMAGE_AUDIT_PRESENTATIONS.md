Reviewed: 2026-08-07
Scope: every non-catalog, self-generated explanatory diagram currently published on the site and in the PDF export, checked against the owner's presentation slides where those slides are available, and otherwise against the approved English text and the already-approved Xinzuo catalog images.

**None of these five files exist anywhere this session could find them**: not in the repository working tree, not on any of the 9 branches on the remote (`main`, `add-pdf-export-action`, `add-visual-learning-assets`, `claude/add-approved-catalog-images-er6psu`, `github-pages-site`, `improve-multilingual-publishing`, `initial-knowledge-base`, `multilingual-auto-deploy`, `rewrite-long-form-v1`), and not anywhere else on this session's filesystem. Per the explicit instruction not to guess, **no image in this pass was replaced with a presentation-slide crop**, because no presentation slide was accessible to crop from. Where a generated diagram failed review and no approved catalog image could substitute for it, it was withdrawn to an `IMAGE PLACEHOLDER` rather than left in place or replaced with a fabricated approximation, per the owner's own stated fallback rule ("it is better to revert to an IMAGE PLACEHOLDER than to keep a wrong image").

**If the five presentation files are placed in the repository (e.g. in a staging folder such as `_incoming/`) in a future session, this audit should be repeated** for the one diagram withdrawn below and for the still-open placeholders in the geometry/bevel and knife-shape sections, since real slide crops are the owner's stated first preference.
